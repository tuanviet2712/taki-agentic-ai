# 10 Template Prompt AI Video (Tiếng Anh)

10 template chuẩn cho 10 loại cảnh phổ biến trong video triết lý. Copy + thay `[bracket]` khi dùng.

---

## Template 1: WALKING / BƯỚC ĐI

**Dùng cho**: Hồi Setup hoặc transition giữa các hồi.

```
Medium shot of a minimalist black stick figure walking slowly 
from right to left across a wide empty white background. 
The figure has a round head with no facial features except two 
small eye dots. Thin black line body, arms swinging gently.
Feet stepping in a steady rhythm.

Style: hand-drawn 2D animation, black ink lines on pure white 
background, whiteboard animation aesthetic, minimalist.

Camera: static wide shot, centered composition.
Lighting: flat even white, no shadows.
Motion: smooth walking cycle, slow pace, contemplative.
Mood: solitary, reflective.
Duration: 3 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical.

Negative prompt: realistic human, 3D, photorealistic, detailed face, 
colorful, cluttered background, cartoon style.
```

## Template 2: SITTING / NGỒI CHIÊM NGHIỆM

**Dùng cho**: Hồi Insight — khoảnh khắc suy tư.

```
Wide shot of a minimalist black stick figure sitting cross-legged

The figure's back is straight, hands resting on knees,

A few simple dashed leaves drifting down around the figure.

Style: hand-drawn 2D line art, black ink on white, 
Zen minimalist illustration, whiteboard aesthetic.

Camera: static wide shot, figure centered, tree as frame.
Lighting: soft warm glow around the figure (subtle golden tint).
Motion: very subtle — leaves falling slowly, figure completely still.
Mood: peaceful, enlightened, meditative.
Duration: 4 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical.

Negative prompt: realistic, 3D, detailed facial expressions, 
colored clothes, photographic, cluttered.
```

## Template 3: STORM / NGÃ DƯỚI BÃO

**Dùng cho**: Hồi Conflict — khoảnh khắc vật lộn.

```
Medium shot of a minimalist black stick figure standing in heavy rain, 
body tilted 30 degrees forward against strong wind. Both hands 
crossed over chest, head bowed low. Rain drawn as diagonal dashed 
black lines filling the frame. A few dried leaves blowing across.
Lightning flash visible at top of frame.

Style: hand-drawn 2D black ink illustration, whiteboard animation, 
dramatic but still minimalist (not realistic).

Camera: static medium shot, slight camera shake for tension.
Lighting: darker background (light gray #E0E0E0), occasional 
lightning flash brightening the scene briefly.
Motion: figure remains tilted but trembling slightly, rain 
falling rapidly at 45-degree angle, leaves flying past.
Mood: tense, vulnerable, but unyielding.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical.

Negative prompt: realistic weather, 3D rain, photorealistic, 
colorful lightning, detailed human, stormy sky with clouds detail.
```

## Nguyên tắc dùng template

### 1. Luôn tùy chỉnh theo storyboard
Template là khởi điểm — điều chỉnh subject, action, mood theo cảnh cụ thể.

### 2. Giữ STYLE nhất quán
Mọi prompt phải có dòng style giống nhau. Nếu cảnh 1 là "whiteboard aesthetic", thì cảnh 5 cũng phải "whiteboard aesthetic".

### 3. Duration khớp storyboard
Tổng duration các prompt = độ dài video yêu cầu.

### 4. Negative prompt bắt buộc
Luôn có để tránh AI render sai style (thành realistic, 3D, colorful...).

### 5. Aspect ratio phải ghi rõ
`9:16 vertical` cho TikTok/Reels, `16:9 horizontal` cho YouTube.

## Cách ghép nhiều cảnh thành 1 video

**Bước 1**: Tạo prompt cho mỗi cảnh (dùng template phù hợp)

**Bước 2**: Render từng cảnh riêng bằng AI tool

**Bước 3**: Ghép lại trong CapCut/Premiere:
- Đặt clips theo thứ tự
- Transition fade 0.3-0.5s
- Thêm voice-over
- Thêm nhạc nền
- Thêm text overlay

**Bước 4**: Export MP4

Xem chi tiết `editing-guide.md`.

## Mẹo prompt engineering nâng cao

### Character consistency (nhất quán nhân vật)
Trong mỗi prompt, dùng cùng 1 mô tả character:
```
"a minimalist black stick figure with round head, no facial features 
except two small eye dots, thin line body"
```
Copy đúng câu này vào mỗi cảnh.

### Scene transition hint
Nếu muốn chuyển cảnh mượt, thêm vào cuối prompt:
```
"Scene ends with camera slowly zooming out / fading to white / 
tilting up to sky."
```
Scene sau bắt đầu bằng:
```
"Scene begins with camera zoomed in from previous shot / fading in 
from white / tilting down from sky."
```

### Tone consistency (nhất quán tông)
Giữ cùng keyword mood xuyên suốt:
- Philosophical: "contemplative, reflective, timeless, serene"
- Emotional: "vulnerable, tender, hopeful"
- Triumphant: "resilient, enlightened, peaceful"
